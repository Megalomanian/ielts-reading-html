Task 1 · 图表小作文

小作文只做三件事：说清图是什么、挑出最值得说的特征、拿数据做有意义的比较。八类图共用一套骨架，差别在于「什么关系最值得写」。每篇范文都在右侧标出对应评分维度的具体证据。

折线图

Line graph考频最高的一类，核心是「方向 + 幅度 + 交叉点」
Task 1 · Academic折线图2026-10-28 考场回忆

三家伦敦博物馆的访客量Visitor numbers at three London museums, 2000–2020

折线图趋势比较三点时间轴建议 20 分钟目标 Band 7.5+来源：湖南开放大学雅思机考考点
题目 Prompt

The graph below shows the number of visitors to three museums in London in 2000, 2010 and 2020. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

数据按考场回忆复原为示意数据，用于练结构，不保证与原卷一致。

下图显示 2000、2010、2020 年伦敦三家博物馆的访客量。
任务要求：选取并报告主要特征，在相关处作比较。
写之前先想清楚：三条线走的是三种完全不同的路径——持续上升、先升后降、持续下降。Overview 就是把这三条路径一句话说清，细节段再拿数字当证据。

范文 Model answer
Model answer · 原创范文176 words

AThe line graph compares the number of visitors to three museums in London at three points in time: 2000, 2010 and 2020.开头：改写题目，只交代「谁、在哪、什么时候、什么单位」，不加任何评价。

BOverall, Museum A attracted a steadily growing audience and finished the period as the most popular of the three, whereas Museum C recorded an uninterrupted decline. Museum B, by contrast, peaked in 2010 before losing visitors.概述：一句话把三条路径全交代完——上升、下降、先升后降。不写任何具体数字。

CIn 2000, Museum B was the leading attraction with 600,000 visitors, followed by Museum A at 500,000 and Museum C at 400,000. Over the following decade, however, A's figure climbed to 650,000 while B rose far more sharply to 900,000. Museum C, meanwhile, slipped to 300,000, already the least visited of the three. A's growth was also the steadiest: the museum added roughly 150,000 visitors in each decade, whereas B's trajectory changed direction twice.细节段一：先给起点排名，再写 2000–2010 的三条变化，用 however / while / meanwhile 把三条线缝在一段里；末句用「每十年稳定增加约 15 万」把 A 与 B 的波动区分开。

DBetween 2010 and 2020 this pattern reversed. B fell back to 700,000 and surrendered first place to A, whose visitor numbers reached 800,000 at the end of the period. C continued its downward path, ending at 200,000, or exactly half its figure for 2000.细节段二：写后半段的排名互换，并用「half its figure for 2000」给结尾一个可计算的对比，而不是干巴巴报数。

评分维度 Scoring
Task Achievement7.5

三个时间点、三条线全部覆盖，且区分了「起点最高」「中途峰值」「终点最高」三个不同身份。Overview 用了 和 两种不同性质的描述，不是笼统说「都变了」。

Coherence & Cohesion7.5

分段按时间切（2000–2010 / 2010–2020），而不是按博物馆切——按主体分会变成三份互不相干的流水账。连接词 however、while、meanwhile、by contrast 都指向真实的逻辑转折；B 这件事被放在概述里先交代，细节段才有地方展开。

Lexical Resource7.5

用 代替 became less popular than，用 代替 decreased to，动词带出了「位置」和「轻微下滑」的差别。数字表达也有变化：600,000 / half its figure for 2000，避免通篇百分比。

Grammatical Range7.5

结尾的 是非限制性定语从句，把「A 超过 B」和「A 的具体数字」压进一句，是 7 分档典型的紧凑写法。

结构拆解 Sentence by sentence
  • 第 1 句改写题目。注意保留了 three points in time 这个设定，因为后面确实只有三个点，含糊说 over the period 反而不准。
  • 第 2 句Overview 前半：A 上升、C 下降。用 体现「稳定」，这是从 500→650→800 的等距增长读出来的。
  • 第 3 句Overview 后半：B 的「先升后降」。peak 是这类双段走势的固定抓手。
  • 第 4 句细节起点。给出三个数字并顺带排出名次，为后文的「换位」埋伏笔。
  • 第 5–6 句2000–2010：A 温和增长、B 暴涨、C 下滑。三句并成一段靠 however / while / meanwhile 三个连接。末句 把 A 的「匀速」量化出来——同样都在涨，A 的形状是一条直线，B 是一条折线，这个差别不说，Overview 里的 steadily 就没有证据。
  • 第 7–9 句2010–2020：B 回落并丢榜首，A 登顶，C 减半。结尾用 half 收一个漂亮的对照。

语法亮点 Grammatical range
  • 非限制性定语从句：A, whose visitor numbers reached 800,000, ... —— 补充信息而不打断主句，7 分常用。
  • 对比状语从句：whereas Museum C recorded ... —— 一句里放两条相反趋势，比分成两句更紧凑。
  • 分词/介词短语收尾：ending at 200,000, or exactly half its figure for 2000 —— 用 or 引出解释性同位语，避免再起一句。
考官视角 Examiner note

这篇最容易被忽略的一点是：它没有把三个博物馆各写一段。按主体分段是 5.5–6 分最常见的结构病，读者读完只知道三家各自发生了什么，不知道它们之间的关系。按时间分段、在每段里横向比，才叫 make comparisons where relevant。另外注意 Overview 里一个数字都没有——概述给的是「形状」，不是「数据」。

常见踩坑 Pitfalls
  • 把 2000、2010、2020 三个点写成三句「In 2000..., In 2010..., In 2020...」，读起来像流水账，Coherence 直接掉档。
  • Overview 里堆数字。概述的任务是让读者先拿到一张地图，数字属于细节段。
  • 把 half its figure for 2000 算错：400,000 → 200,000 才是减半，别套到 B 身上。
  • 通篇用 increase / decrease。这两个词没错，但重复四次以上就是词汇不足的信号。

柱状图

Bar chart分组柱状图最大的坑是按活动一组一段写成流水账——先按「升 / 降」分组，再在组内比幅度和名次
Task 1 · Academic柱状图2026-10-27 考场回忆

成人参与四项休闲活动的比例Percentage of adults taking part in four leisure activities, 2005 and 2015

柱状图两组年份有升有降名次变化建议 20 分钟目标 Band 7.5+来源：台北市農會信義大樓
题目 Prompt

The bar chart below shows the percentage of adults in a country who took part in four leisure activities in 2005 and 2015. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

数据按考场回忆复原为示意数据，用于练结构，不保证与原卷一致。

下图显示某国 2005 与 2015 年参与四项休闲活动的成人比例。
任务要求：选取并报告主要特征，在相关处作比较。
写之前先想清楚：四项活动两项涨、两项跌，而且第一名换了人——阅读从 45% 跌到 34%，不再是最普及的活动。Overview 要把「哪两组」和「谁取代了谁」一起说清，细节段再按升降分组给数字。

范文 Model answer
Model answer · 原创范文179 words

AThe bar chart compares the percentage of adults taking part in four leisure activities in 2005 and 2015: reading, gardening, team sports and online gaming.开头：改写题目。四项活动放到冒号后列举，主句不被四个名词撑长。

BOverall, team sports and online gaming gained ground, whereas reading and gardening lost it. The change was big enough to reverse the ranking: team sports, third in 2005, had become the most common activity by 2015.概述：先按方向把四项分成两组，再补一句名次反转。全段不出现任何数字。

CIn 2005, 22% of adults played team sports and only 12% played online games, the lowest figure on the chart. Both grew sharply over the next ten years. Team sports rose to 40%, close to doubling, while online gaming climbed to 35%, almost tripling, so that it was no longer a minority interest.细节段一：只写上升的两项。先给起点，再一句总起，最后用两个倍数完成组内比较。

DReading and gardening moved the other way, though neither collapsed. The proportion of adults who read fell from 45% to 34%, which cost reading its position at the top of the chart, and gardening declined more gently, from 38% to 28%. Even so, the four activities ended the period within twelve percentage points of one another, compared with a spread of 33 points in 2005.细节段二：下降的两项，先写跌幅大的阅读并点出丢了第一名，再写跌幅小的园艺，最后用极差收一个整体判断。

评分维度 Scoring
Task Achievement7.5

四项活动、两个年份全部覆盖，而且在 Overview 里抓住了这道题真正的 main feature：不只是「两项升两项降」，还有第一名换人——2005 年最高的阅读到 2015 年只排第三。只写方向不写名次，Task Achievement 一般停在 7；把名次反转写出来，才够 7.5。另外结尾用「极差从 33 点缩到 12 点」把四个数字当成一个整体看，而不是四份独立报告。

Coherence & Cohesion7.5

分段按变化方向切：上升组一段、下降组一段，读者拿到的是两条相反的走势。这也让 和 能在同一段里互为参照。若改成 Reading 一段、Gardening 一段、Team sports 一段、Online gaming 一段，段与段之间就没有任何比较关系，Coherence 会掉到 6——这正是 这种成对动词能省下的结构成本。

Lexical Resource7.5

倍数表达不重复： 配 22%→40%， 配 12%→35%，都按实际倍数选词，没有一律说 increased。 把「跌 10 点」和「跌 11 点」的细微差别写出来，比两个 decreased 有信息。 把「从第一掉下来」写成一个因果小句，而不是 reading decreased and was no longer the highest。

Grammatical Range7.5

Overview 第二句 team sports, third in 2005, had become the most common activity by 2015 用了插入语加过去完成时：插入语省掉一个定语从句，by 2015 又把「先三后一」的先后关系标清楚。结尾 是介词短语做范围限定，比 all four were similar 精确，也不必另起一句。

结构拆解 Sentence by sentence
  • 第 1 句改写题目。四项活动放在冒号后列举，是为了让 the percentage of adults taking part in four leisure activities 这个长主语先落地，列完再收年份。
  • 第 2 句Overview 前半： / lost it 一对动词就把四项分成两组，比分别说 two increased and two decreased 更省字，也自带对比。
  • 第 3 句Overview 后半：补名次反转。这里只说 third 和 the most common，不给数字——概述给形状，细节给数据。
  • 第 4–5 句细节段一先给 2005 的两个起点（22%、12%），再用 Both grew sharply 一句总起，避免「先报一个数、再报一个数」的流水账节奏。
  • 第 6 句上升组的核心： 与 并置，一句话完成组内比较；so that 从句顺带交代了 12% 这个「少数派」身份的消失。
  • 第 7–8 句下降组复制同一结构：先起点 45% / 38%，再给终点。which 从句专写阅读丢榜首的后果， 则把园艺的跌幅差异标出来。
  • 第 9 句收尾不重复数字，而是把两列数据换算成「极差」：33 点缩到 12 点。 这种说法考官看的是你有没有把四个数字当整体读。

语法亮点 Grammatical range
  • 冒号列举：four leisure activities in 2005 and 2015: reading, gardening, team sports and online gaming —— 列举甩到句末，主句保持短，读起来不喘。
  • 过去完成时标先后：team sports, third in 2005, had become the most common activity by 2015 —— by + 过去时间点是过去完成时的固定信号，用来写「到某年为止已发生」。

考官视角 Examiner note

这篇最容易被忽略的判断是：柱状图的 main feature 往往是「谁取代了谁」，而不是「谁涨谁跌」。方向只是第一层，2005 年第一名（阅读 45%）到 2015 年跌到第三（34%）、团队运动从第三升到第一，这才是这道题独有的信息。只写两项升两项降，任何一道双向柱状图都能套用，考官一眼看出是背模板，Task Achievement 停在 7。另外注意最后一句的极差：它证明作者把四个数字当成一个分布在看，而不是四条互不相干的新闻。

常见踩坑 Pitfalls
  • 按活动分四段，每段一句「X rose from ... to ...」：四项之间零比较，Coherence 直接 6 分。
  • Overview 里写 22%→40%：把概述写成第二个细节段，等于浪费了唯一一次「先给结论」的机会。
  • 把 12%→35% 说成 tripled：实际是 2.9 倍，倍数说错在 Task Achievement 上算数据误读，比用词平淡更致命。
  • 只写升降、不写名次，或者反过来只写「阅读最受欢迎」而不看 2015 年——单一年份的排名不能当结论。

饼图

Pie chart饼图最容易死在「逐张图把每个扇区报一遍」——三张图的意义在于同一条扇区在三年的移动，不在每张图内部

度假者住宿方式的三个年份Accommodation chosen by holidaymakers in 1988, 1998 and 2008

饼图三个饼份额转移首尾对比建议 20 分钟目标 Band 7.5+来源：杭州电子科技大学雅思机考考点
题目 Prompt

The pie charts below show the accommodation chosen by holidaymakers in 1988, 1998 and 2008. Summarise the information by selecting and reporting the main features, and make comparisons where relevant.

数据按考场回忆复原为示意数据，用于练结构，不保证与原卷一致。

下图显示 1988、1998、2008 年度假者选择的住宿方式。
任务要求：选取并报告主要特征，在相关处作比较。
写之前先想清楚：三张饼是同一个整体的三次快照，百分比加总都是 100。所以一间酒店少掉的 18 个点，必然变成别人的增长——把「谁让出来、谁接过去」写清楚，比报四遍数字有用得多。

范文 Model answer
Model answer · 原创范文189 words

AThe pie charts show the accommodation that holidaymakers chose in 1988, 1998 and 2008, divided into four types: hotels, camping, staying with friends and rented flats.开头：改写题目。三个年份并列举完，再用 divided into 把四种住宿方式挂上去。

BOverall, the share taken by hotels fell in each of the three years, while camping and rented flats became steadily more common. Staying with friends was the one option that hardly moved, and hotels remained the largest single choice even in 2008.概述：一句话给三条轨迹（持续跌 / 持续涨 / 基本不动），再补一句「跌了还是第一」，防止读者误以为酒店出局。

CIn 1988, hotels accounted for 48% of holidaymakers, more than double the share of any other option. Ten years later that figure had dropped to 40%, and by 2008 it stood at 30%. The decline of eighteen percentage points was the largest single change across the three charts, yet hotels still housed almost one holidaymaker in three.细节段一：只追酒店一条线，三个年份连读，中间用 1988 年的「超过其余三项之和」给起点一个分量。

DThe growth was spread across the cheaper options rather than concentrated in one. Camping rose from 16% to 26% between 1988 and 2008, an increase of ten points, and rented flats did much the same, from 14% to 20%, with most of that gain arriving after 1998. Staying with friends, by contrast, hardly changed, moving only from 22% to 24% over the two decades.细节段二：把剩下三项放在一起，用「涨幅分散」这个判断串起来，并用首尾年份直接对比。

评分维度 Scoring
Task Achievement7.5

三张饼、四种住宿方式全部覆盖，但真正的分数来自两处判断：一是把 当成一条跨年份的线来读，而不是每年报一个数；二是结尾的 承认有一项几乎没动——很多考生为了让文章「有变化」，会把 22%→24% 硬说成 rose slightly，反而暴露没读准。首尾年份的 也做了直接对比，符合题目的 make comparisons 要求。

Coherence & Cohesion7.5

分段按「主体」切而非按「年份」切：一段专写酒店，一段写其余三项。若按年份分三段（1988 一段、1998 一段、2008 一段），每段都要重复四个数字，读者读完仍不知道趋势，这是饼图最常见的 6 分结构。段内的连接也承担信息：Ten years later、by 2008、with most of that gain arriving after 1998 把时间点串成线，by contrast 则把「几乎不动」从「增长」里明确摘出来。

Lexical Resource7.5

动词按主体分工：酒店用 accounted for / dropped / ，营地用 rose，租屋用 did much the same 避免第二个 rose。 是饼图独有的比较方式——它同时把「第一名的分量」和「其余各项都远低于它」写进一句话，柱状图里很难这么比。 把「三项都在涨」压缩成一个判断，比分别说 three options increased 有解释力。

Grammatical Range7.5

时间跨度用不同时态分层：In 1988 hotels accounted for（一般过去）→ Ten years later that figure had dropped（过去完成）→ by 2008 it stood at（一般过去 + by 时间点）。这不是炫技，而是三张饼必须解决的先后问题。 是名词化同位语，把 16%→26% 的差值单独拎出来，比 and it increased by ten points 少一个分句。

网络看展之后，博物馆还有必要吗Will museums and art galleries still be needed?

观点类科技替代文化与公共机构立场边界建议 40 分钟目标 Band 7.5+来源：IDP雅思广州天河区考点
题目 Prompt

Nowadays, some people claim that public museums and art galleries will not be needed because people can see historical objects and works of art by using a computer. To what extent do you agree or disagree?

题目给出一个「技术替代」的判断：既然电脑上能看到文物和艺术品，公共博物馆与美术馆就不再必要。
任务要求：回答 to what extent，即同意的程度，并说明理由。
写之前先想清楚：to what extent 允许你限定同意范围，但必须给出一个能站住的边界——比如「数字复制解决不了实物在场的体验」。边界一旦定下，让步段也只能让到这条线为止，不能顺手承认「所以博物馆确实可以取消」。

范文 Model answer
Model answer · 原创范文297 words

AIt is often argued that museums and art galleries will no longer be needed once historical objects and art can be viewed on a computer. I partly disagree: digital images are a real gain, but they replace the circulation of pictures, not the encounter with an object.开头：两句话完成改写 + 表态，并把「不同意」限定在「实物相遇不可替代」这一条边界上。

BThe strongest argument for the screen is access. A student in a town without a gallery can examine a scroll in greater detail than a visitor pressed against a glass case, at no cost and at any hour. Museums that digitise their holdings therefore widen their audience rather than lose it; for people who cannot travel, the screen is not a poor substitute but the only one.让步段：认真承认对方最强的一条理由——数字化扩大了可达性，而且对无法出行的人来说是唯一选择。

CA reproduction is fixed by someone else's decisions. The photographer chooses the angle and the lighting, so what reaches the screen is an interpretation of the object rather than the object itself. In a gallery the visitor controls those variables: walking around a sculpture shows how its weight is carried, and standing beside a canvas reveals a scale that a laptop flattens. Originals also carry evidence of age that no photograph records.主体一：机制——复制品被他人的视角固定，实物允许观者自己控制角度、距离和尺度。

DMuseums also do what no archive can: they decide what a public ought to see. A search returns only what the user already suspects exists, whereas a curator sets unfamiliar work beside familiar work and forces a comparison. A class taken through a gallery therefore learns something a list of links cannot teach, and free entry keeps that open to anyone who walks in.主体二：机制——博物馆的功能是公共策展，把观者没在找的东西摆到他面前，这是搜索做不到的。

EMuseums will change, and should: putting collections online reaches people a ticket price never will. But to conclude that they are therefore unnecessary confuses information with encounter. As long as a city wants original objects kept, shown together and explained in public, the gallery has a purpose.结尾：先承认会变、该上网，再点出对方推理的错误，最后用条件句把立场收在边界上。

Task Response7.5

双边讨论环境与商业双边论证条件式立场建议 40 分钟目标 Band 7.5+来源：British Council 广州雅思机考考点
题目 Prompt

Some people think climate change has a negative impact on business, while others think that it provides more business opportunities. Discuss both views and give your own opinion.

题目给出两种对立判断：气候变化损害商业，或者它带来更多商业机会。
任务要求：讨论双方观点，并给出自己的意见——三件事都要做，缺一件就掉分。
写之前先想清楚：discuss both views 要求两方篇幅大致对等，而 own opinion 必须单独落到某一段或结尾，不能只在开头敷衍一句。最省力的做法是给双方各自一个成立条件，再让自己的立场去解释「什么条件下哪一方占上风」。

范文 Model answer
Model answer · 原创范文296 words

AClimate change is now described both as a risk that firms must survive and as a market that firms can enter. This essay examines both readings before arguing that which one holds depends on the horizon a company works to.开头：一句并列改写题目里的两种说法（risk / market），第二句预告立场是「看时间尺度」，只说 depends on，不在这里给条件。

BThe pessimistic case rests on cost and disruption. Extreme weather closes ports and factories, insurance premiums climb for exposed sites, and carbon pricing charges every tonne emitted. For sectors tied to a fixed location, such as farming and coastal property, the arithmetic is harsh: the physical base of the business is being eroded, and no efficiency saving recovers it.观点一：威胁论。给的是成本机制——天气、保费、碳价三重挤压，最后用「选址无法搬走」收口。

CThe optimistic case points to the demand that the same pressure creates. A levy that makes emissions expensive opens a market for low-carbon substitutes, and a firm that redesigns its product early can charge a premium and attract capital that rivals cannot. Renewable installation and grid repair exist because the old arrangement became costly. On this reading, a constraint on one business model is a vacancy for another.观点二：机会论。机制是「监管制造需求」——同一部法规既抬高高碳成本，也腾出低碳市场。

DIn my view these are less rivals than answers to different questions about time. Over one to three years, adaptation is pure cost: equipment is replaced and margins narrow. Over ten to fifteen years, the firms that invested early set the standard that others must then buy into. What separates the two is whether a business can reinvest before the physical risk arrives, rather than treating each levy as a one-off charge.自己的立场：不是二选一，而是按时间尺度分。短期是成本，长期是机会，真正的变量是能否在风险到来前再投资。

EThe honest answer, then, is conditional: climate change is a threat to firms whose assets are fixed and whose planning stops at the next reporting cycle, and an opportunity to those able to fund a transition. Which group grows larger depends less on the climate itself than on how quickly policy makes the cost of delay visible.结尾：把立场写成条件句——资产固定且只看短周期的是威胁，能融资转型的是机会；最后一句把责任推回政策设计。

范文 Model answer
Model answer · 原创范文299 words

APlanners in many cities keep shops, schools, offices and housing in separate districts, assuming each use works best among its own kind. BThe case for separation rests on friction between uses. Keeping such traffic on a few arterial roads protects the streets behind them, and a child can then walk to school without crossing a commercial junction.利的一段：先说机制（用途之间有摩擦），再指出受损的是最走不开的人，最后给出具体手段（主干道）。

CThe cost is distance, and distance is paid every day. DSet against this, the advantage shrinks, because quiet has cheaper substitutes. EZoning therefore fails both halves of my test. Its benefit reaches a minority and could be secured by cheaper means, while its cost falls on nearly everyone, every day. Planners would do better to mix uses and manage the friction between them.结论：用开头的同一把尺子宣判，最后半句给替代方案，避免结尾只剩 repeat。

CReward, however, explains only part of it. Some people work hard because their sense of professional identity will not allow them to submit anything second-rate. A teacher who rewrites a lesson plan at midnight is not chasing a bonus; the standard has been internalised, and falling short of it feels like a personal failure. This perfectionism is a motive distinct from ambition, and it survives when nobody is watching.原因二（内在标准）：职业认同与完美主义，用教师改教案的具体画面支撑，明确与「想要奖励」区分开。

DYet hard work is not automatically good. Beyond a certain point, extra hours bring diminishing returns: a tired employee makes mistakes that a rested one avoids, and a student who studies until three in the morning retains less than one who stops at eleven. Chronic overwork also damages health. More subtly, effort can become an end in itself, so that people judge themselves by hours spent rather than by what those hours achieve; at that point diligence works against the goals it was meant to serve.评价段：先否定「总是」，再给三条边界——边际递减、健康代价、把努力本身当目的。

EIn short, people work hard for external reward and for internal standards, but the value of that effort depends on what it produces and what it costs them.结尾：两问各收一句，用「产出与代价」给出判断标准，不喊口号。

评分维度 Scoring
Task Response7.5

两问各有一笔清楚的账：第 2 段用 解释外在激励，第 3 段用 解释内在标准，两条原因性质不同，所以第一问不是把同一句话说两遍。第二问没有停在「有好处也有坏处」，而是用 和 说清「从哪一刻起不再划算」，正面回答了 always 这个词。开头那句 提前把这两个标准摆出来，所以第 4 段的三条边界不是临时凑的，而是兑现开头的承诺。

Coherence & Cohesion7.5

五段的顺序就是题目的问句顺序：现象 → 原因一 → 原因二 → 评价 → 收束。第 3 段开头 however 是关键信号，它告诉读者「换的是原因的类型，不是再加一个原因」；第 4 段用 Beyond a certain point、More subtly 层层推进，而不是并列罗列。若把两问压进一段，连接词就会从「推进」退化成 and also，读者分不清哪里是回答哪一问。

Lexical Resource7.5

词汇集中在一个语义场里：priced、cheapest route、diminishing returns、costs，都在谈「划算不划算」，所以读起来是一篇有统一视角的文章，不是好词拼盘。 比 the easiest way to succeed 多一层成本含义，正好服务第一问的经济解释；second-rate 这种带评价的形容词也比 not good 更能体现「内在标准」。

Grammatical Range7.5

第 3 段 用现在完成被动，强调标准已经变成一种内部状态，而不是谁在要求；第 2 段 Where reward and effort are tightly linked 用 where 引导条件，等于 in cases where，比 if 更书面。全文没有一句超过 45 词的失控长句，复杂句都控制在能一次读懂的长度。

结构拆解 Sentence by sentence
  • 第 1 段两句分工：第一句立现象（有人投入更多），第二句交代文章怎么处理两个问句。注意它没有写 This essay will discuss both views，因为这不是 discuss both views 题。
  • 第 2 段第 1 句 一句立论，把「为什么更努力」直接翻译成「努力有价格」，后面两句都只是给这个隐喻补证据。
  • 第 2 段第 2–3 句职场与教育各一例，证明同一机制在两个场域都成立。只写公司会显得眼界窄，只写学生会漏掉 employment 这个词。
  • 第 2 段第 4 句条件句收束：只有奖励与努力挂钩时，努力才是最便宜的路。加上这层条件，原因才不是绝对化的「人都贪财」。
  • 第 3 段第 1 句however 转折，明确「钱只解释一部分」，把第二类原因单独起段。这一句是两问之外、第一问内部的分层信号。
  • 第 3 段第 3 句教师半夜改教案的具体画面，替代 hard-working、dedicated 这类形容词；后半句 the standard has been internalised 点明标准来自内部。
  • 第 4 段评价段的骨架：先否定 always（not automatically good），再按「产出递减 → 健康 → 目的置换」三层加深，最后一层最抽象也最致命，放在段尾。
  • 第 5 段结尾不重复两个原因，只把它们压成 external reward / internal standards 两个短语，然后用 depends on what it produces and what it costs 交出判断标准。

考官视角 Examiner note

这篇最容易被忽略的判断在第 4 段：它没有把第二问做成「好处一段、坏处一段」的利弊清单。题目问的是 is it always a good thing，always 要的是边界，不是两边各让一步。所以这一段先承认 ，再补健康代价，最后用 指出努力从手段变成了目的——三条都在回答「什么时候这个说法不成立」。反过来，写 on the one hand it brings success, on the other hand it causes stress 的考生看上去很平衡，实际上始终没碰 always，Task Response 通常卡在 6。

常见踩坑 Pitfalls
  • 只回答第二问。第一问用 people work hard because they want to succeed 一句带过，等于主动放弃一半任务，TR 很难上 6。
  • 两问合成一段，各写两句。表面上面面俱到，实际上两个原因都没展开、评价也没给条件，是最典型的「都浅」。
  • 两个原因其实是一个原因：既写「想赚钱」又写「想成功」，本质都是外在回报，读者会觉得第 3 段是第 2 段的复述。
  • 第二问写成利弊清单却不回答 always。列出三个好处三个坏处，但没有说清「到哪一步就不再是好事」，仍然算没答完。
  • 结尾喊口号：so we should work hard and also take care of our health。这是建议，不是对「是否总是好事」的判断。
