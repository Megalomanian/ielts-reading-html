## 二、人物类

### P1 · 26 岁的 AI 创业者

**题目**：a person who impressed you / a successful young person / someone you would like to meet

**关键词**

- a twenty-something founder
- pitched at a whiteboard, no slides
- ten minutes, everyone understood
- computing-subsidy review meeting
- an open-plan floor shared by fifty start-ups
- answered the hardest question first
- calm about risk, specific about money

待补：他的产品方向（一句话）。

**加分表达**

- `He explained something complicated without making anyone in the room feel stupid.`
- `What struck me wasn't the idea — it was how relaxed he was about failing.`
- `He talked about failure as a schedule, not a disaster.`
- `He disagreed with people without making it personal.`
- `That was the first time I saw a company as a series of decisions rather than a fact.`

## 三、地点类

### L1 · 威尔士书镇 Hay-on-Wye（未去过）

**题目**：a place you would like to visit / a quiet place / a place you would recommend

**关键词**

- a small town on the border of Wales and England
- about two thousand residents, around twenty bookshops
- grey stone, narrow streets, permanent drizzle
- an honesty bookshop inside a castle ruin — shelves outdoors, an iron box for coins
- a cinema in an old chapel
- one shop selling only maps, another only poetry
- a literary festival every May, tens of thousands of visitors
- I have only seen it in a documentary

待补：你想去的具体理由（一句话，例如「想闻旧纸味」）。

**加分表达**

- `Twenty bookshops for two thousand people — the maths doesn't work, and that's the whole point.`
- `You take a book, and you put the money in an iron box, and nobody checks.`
- `It's the kind of place that makes you lower your voice without being asked.`
- `It looks like a town that decided to stop competing with the rest of the world.`
- `I've never stood in it, and that's exactly why I keep describing it.`

## 四、事件类

### E1 · BERT 评论满意度系统

**题目**：a project you worked on / a time you led a team / something you are proud of

**关键词**

- December 2024 to June 2025
- five students, very different levels
- fine-tuned BERT-wwm-ext on Chinese reviews
- Flask API, Bootstrap front end, Docker
- noisy labels: two people reading the same comment, disagreeing
- looked fine on training data, fell apart on real reviews
- two weeks arguing about labelling rules
- re-labelled a subset, added data augmentation
- final presentation: juniors explained their own code

待补：准确率前后两个数字。

**加分表达**

- `Half my job was technical, and the other half was just people.`
- `The hardest decision wasn't which model to use — it was admitting the data was the problem.`
- `We spent two weeks doing something that felt like nothing: arguing about labels.`
- `We didn't fix the model first; we fixed the labels.`
- `I stopped being the person who wrote the most code and became the person who unblocked everyone else.`
- `What I'm proud of isn't the accuracy. It's that the youngest member could explain the architecture without me.`

## 五、物品类

### O1 · 跑模型的那台旧笔记本（使用角度）

**题目**：a piece of technology you use often / something you fixed / something you would hate to lose

**关键词**

- a second-hand laptop, bought before the project started
- a dent in one corner, a club sticker on the lid
- the fan went up an octave the moment training began
- ran eleven hours straight, twice, and survived
- battery dead by the third month, permanently plugged in
- carried it to every meeting, weighed down by the charger
- it ran the final demo in front of the class
- still on my desk, still working

待补：价格或使用年限（两个数字）。

**加分表达**

- `You could hear it thinking — the fan told you when the model was actually learning.`
- `It's not an expensive machine; it's just a machine that refused to give up.`
- `Five people pushed it to its limit for six months, and it never once lost my data.`
- `The sticker on the lid is the only decoration, and the only proof it's mine.`
- `Throwing it away would feel like deleting the project.`

### G1 · 那台笔记本（礼物角度）

**题目**：a gift you received / something expensive you own / something you use every day

**关键词**

- a laptop, given to me before university started
- not the newest model, but chosen carefully
- the moment it was handed over, still in the box
- a dent in one corner now, a sticker on the lid
- it ran my first project, my first internship application, my first all-nighter
- battery dead by the third month, permanently plugged in

待补：谁送的 + 大概价格或那年型号。

**加分表达**

- `It wasn't a surprise, and that made it mean more — someone had thought about it for weeks.`
- `The person who gave it to me didn't say much. They just said, "use it properly."`
- `It's the most expensive thing I own and the least interesting thing I own.`
- `Everything I've built in the last two years went through that keyboard.`
- `I'd rather repair it than replace it, and I know that's not rational.`

**注意**：O1 与 G1 是同一台机器。O1 讲它怎么工作、我怎么修；G1 讲谁给的、为什么给、给了之后我做了什么。考试中两条答案不得互相矛盾。

## 九、规则类

### R1 · 授权渗透测试的边界

**题目**：a rule you have to follow / an important law / a rule in your school or workplace / something you learned about responsibility

**关键词**

- an authorised security assessment, April to May 2026
- a written scope list, signed before any work started
- scope, permission, disclosure, remediation
- no testing outside the agreed systems, not even to prove a point
- every finding logged, nothing kept for later
- reporting a vulnerability instead of using it

待补：一次你差点越界、最后停手的时刻。

**加分表达**

- `The rule isn't there to slow you down; it's there so the work can exist at all.`

- `The most important skill isn't breaking in — it's knowing where to stop.`
- `We had permission to look, and absolutely no permission to keep what we found.`
- `I understand the law better from one month of authorised work than from any lecture.`

### 仍然覆盖不到的题型

| 缺口 | 典型题目 | 处理方式 |
|---|---|---|
| 自然与环境 | a place in nature / an environmental problem | 可用 L1 的「雨、石墙、山谷」过渡，但容易显得假 |
| 动物 | a pet / an animal you like | 需要补一句真实经历 |
| 食物与商店 | a meal you enjoyed / a shop you like | 可与 L1 的书店勉强衔接 |
| 交通与延误 | a journey that was delayed / a time you were late | 需要补一件小事故 |
| 运动与健康 | a sport you play / a healthy habit | T1 可勉强用「熬夜做题」，方向不对 |
| 一次争吵 | an argument with a friend | 需要补一句真实经历 |

**结论**：九个素材大致覆盖季度题库的八成。追求百分之百覆盖意义不大——Part 2 允许合理迁移，考官看的是你能不能把一件事讲清楚。

## 十一、使用要点

1. **一个素材只保留一个主角度**，其余角度准备到「能接住追问」为止，避免九个答案讲成同一套话。
2. **事件类讲「决定」，物品类讲「处境」**：同一个 BERT 项目，事件问你做了什么选择，物品问你为什么还留着它。
3. **每篇固定两个数字**：两千人、二十家书店；十一小时、六个月；八岁、三年——开场就说出来。
4. **每题只改三处**：开场句扣题目关键词、结尾扣题句、一到两个例子。中间细节不动。
5. **不要背全文**。背全文会变成朗读，流利度分数反而下降。只背骨架和五个细节词。
